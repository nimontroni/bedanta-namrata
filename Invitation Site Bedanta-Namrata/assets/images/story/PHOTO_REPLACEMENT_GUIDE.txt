HOW TO REPLACE WITH YOUR REAL PHOTOS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A (Easiest):
1. Prepare your 6 real couple photos.
2. Rename them to match the filenames:
   - first_connection.png
   - getting_to_know.png
   - first_meet.png
   - love_begin.png
   - proposal.png
   - infinite.png
3. Overwrite the files in this folder `assets/images/story/`.
4. Refresh your browser (Ctrl + F5 or Cmd + Shift + R). Done!

Option B:
If you save them with new names (e.g. bedanta_namrata_1.jpg), simply update
the "image" paths in:
- `data/content.json`
- `data/content-fallback.js`
- `js/story.js`
